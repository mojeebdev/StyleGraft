[ THE DIFFERENCE ]

Not “copy this website.”
Understand why it works.

Most extractors stop at hex codes, font sizes and border radii. StyleGraft reads the composition underneath: rhythm, density, image treatment, hierarchy, tension and the deliberate imperfections that give a design its character.

Then it filters those decisions through your product, audience and codebase—keeping the useful principles while leaving the original brand, content and identity behind.
