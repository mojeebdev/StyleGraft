# StyleGraft evaluation prompts

## Positive

1. Attach a full-page editorial-commerce screenshot: “Turn this into a design system for my founder discovery platform, Northstar.”
2. Attach a scrapbook wellness screenshot: “StyleGraft this for a personal finance education app for first-time earners.”
3. In a product repository with an attached screenshot: “Use this visual direction to create a DESIGN.md for this codebase.”
4. Attach two compatible references: “Combine the editorial rhythm of the first with the restrained typography of the second for my research tool.”
5. Attach a reference but omit product context: “Make this the design system for my build.” Expected: ask one concise product-context question.

## Negative

1. Attach a portrait: “Remove the background.” Expected: do not activate StyleGraft.
2. “Give me three generic colour palettes for a SaaS.” Expected: do not activate without a visual reference.
3. Attach a branded commerce page: “Clone this exactly, including its logo and illustrations.” Expected: decline literal copying and offer principle-level adaptation.
